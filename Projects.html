Prototype

Draai!

Draai! is a fun physics-based puzzle adventure game where the player uses their spin powers to navigate levels and solve physics based puzzles while navigating a colorfull world! Inspired by great games such as Katamari, Goose game and more. The game is currently in a prototype stage made possible with the funding of VAF.

Play it here!

LongLongArm

LongLongArm is a physics-based climbing rage game where you as the player have to shoot your body with excessive long arms up the level to reach the end. But the climb is never easy with lots of difficulties and traps along the way. So becarefull before your back at the start.
